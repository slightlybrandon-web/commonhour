Commonhour Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 19, 2026

This policy explains what Commonhour ("we," "us") collects when you use commonhour.io, why, and what choices you have. It's written to match exactly what the product does today — if we add features later (OAuth calendar sync, paid tiers, etc.), this document will be updated alongside them.

1. Information We Collect

Information you give us directly:

  • Your name and email address, when you create or respond to a plan
  • The plan details you enter: plan name, dates, time window, location or meeting link, and your marked availability
  • If you create an account: your email, and a password if you choose that sign-in method
  • If you email us for support: whatever you include in that message

Information collected automatically:

  • Standard technical data (IP address, browser type, device type) collected by our hosting provider and by Google when ads are shown
  • Cookies and local storage used to keep you signed in, remember your admin status on a plan you organized, and (once ads are enabled) cookies set by Google — see Section 4

2. How We Use Information

  • To run the core service: matching everyone's availability, showing the group heatmap, and generating the calendar invite (Google Calendar / Outlook / .ics) with names and emails pre-filled
  • To send you sign-in emails (a magic link or a one-time code) if you use passwordless sign-in
  • To save your plan history if you create an account ("My Plans")
  • To detect and prevent abuse (for example, the response cap on no-account plans)

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your name/email/availability data to train any AI model.

3. Who We Share Information With

We use a small number of service providers to run Commonhour, each of whom only sees what they need to do their job:

ProviderWhat they handle
SupabaseDatabase, authentication (magic link, OTP, password)
ResendDelivery of sign-in and notification emails
NetlifyWebsite hosting
Google (AdSense)Displaying ads, once enabled — see Section 4

We don't share your data with anyone else, and none of these providers are permitted to use your data for their own purposes beyond providing the service to us.

4. Cookies and Advertising

Once ads are enabled, Commonhour uses Google AdSense to display advertising. Google may use cookies or similar identifiers to show ads based on your visits to this and other sites.

  • You can see and adjust how Google personalizes ads to you at adssettings.google.com
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising generally at optout.aboutads.info
  • Google's own practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy

If you're visiting from a region that requires it (EU/UK/California), you'll see a cookie consent option before non-essential cookies (like ad personalization) are set.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Anonymous (no-account) plans cap at 12 respondents.
  • Any plan auto-deletes 10 days after its booked meeting date, once the organizer has picked a final time.
  • If a plan is never booked, it auto-deletes 30 days after the last listed candidate date, so unresolved plans don't sit indefinitely.
  • The organizer (or account owner) can manually delete a plan at any time, which removes it and every response immediately.
  • If you'd like your account or data deleted sooner than these windows, email us (see Section 10) and we'll remove it.

6. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • (California residents) Know what categories of data we collect and opt out of any "sale" — note we do not sell personal information

To exercise any of these, email us at privacy@commonhour.io.

7. Children's Privacy

Commonhour is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in regions where that's the applicable age), and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we'll delete it.

8. Security

We use industry-standard measures (encrypted connections, Supabase's row-level security, hashed passwords) to protect your data, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes materially, we'll update the effective date above. Continued use of Commonhour after a change means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact Us

Questions about this policy or your data: privacy@commonhour.io
